🔧 THE 6-STEP AI ENGINE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. MACRO FILTER (25% weight)
   Input: CPI, WTI oil, geopolitical risk, S&P 500
   Process: Score each factor, sum weighted impact
   Output: Macro outlook (Bearish/Neutral/Bullish) + score
   Example: CPI 3.3% (-25), Oil $104.41 (-20), Geo "CRITICAL" (-20) = -65 = BEARISH

2. TECHNICAL CONFIRMATION (30% weight)
   Input: Price, RSI, MACD, SMAs, ADX, Death Cross pattern
   Process: Compare price to support/resistance, momentum analysis
   Output: Tech outlook (Bearish/Neutral/Bullish) + score
   Example: Price below SMA200 (-25), RSI 38 oversold (+30), Death Cross (-20) = -15 = NEUTRAL

3. ON-CHAIN & SENTIMENT (30% weight)
   Input: MVRV Z-Score, SOPR, exchange reserves, Fear & Greed index
   Process: Evaluate holder behavior and market psychology
   Output: On-chain outlook (Bearish/Neutral/Bullish) + score
   Example: MVRV 0.55 fair (+25), SOPR 1.35 accumulation (+20), FG 12 extreme (+25) = +70 = BULLISH

4. DERIVATIVES ANALYSIS (15% weight)
   Input: Funding rates, open interest, liquidation levels
   Process: Calculate leveraged positioning and squeeze risk
   Output: Derivative outlook (Neutral/Squeeze Likely) + score + squeeze %
   Example: Funding -0.021 shorts (+30), OI $24.2B (+15) = +45 = Squeeze Risk 74%

5. SIGNAL COMPUTATION
   Input: All four scores with their weights
   Process: totalScore = macro(0.25) + tech(0.30) + onchain(0.30) + deriv(0.15)
   Output: Signal (BUY/SELL/HOLD) + confidence (0-92%)
   Special: If FG<15 AND funding<-0.015 AND price near support → Force BUY
   Example: totalScore = -16.25 + (-4.5) + 21 + 6.75 = 7 → BUY @ 67% confidence

6. HUMAN-READABLE OUTPUT
   Generate:
   - Prediction: Plain text market forecast
   - Action: Specific trader instruction with entry/target/stop
   - Entry zone: Dollar range (support ±1%)
   - Target zone: Dollar range (resistance ±1%)
   - Stop loss: Below support by 2%
   - R:R ratio: Target / Entry / Stop math
   - Reasoning: 4-5 bullets from top factors
   - Warnings: Macro risks flagged
