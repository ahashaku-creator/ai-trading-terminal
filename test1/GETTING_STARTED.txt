HOW THE AI WORKS (6 STEPS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every 5 minutes, the AI analyzes Bitcoin through 6 steps:

STEP 1 — MACRO FILTER (25% weight)
Asks: "Is the macro environment positive or negative?"
Checks:
  ✓ CPI rate (inflation) — if >3%, that's bearish
  ✓ WTI oil price — if >$90, that's risk-off
  ✓ Geopolitical risk — any critical events?
  ✓ S&P 500 — stocks up or down?
Scores each factor, gives macro outlook: BEARISH/NEUTRAL/BULLISH

STEP 2 — TECHNICAL CONFIRMATION (30% weight)
Asks: "Is the chart showing buy or sell signals?"
Checks:
  ✓ Price vs moving averages — above or below SMAs?
  ✓ RSI indicator — oversold (<30) or overbought (>70)?
  ✓ MACD — positive or negative momentum?
  ✓ ADX — is there a strong trend?
  ✓ Death Cross — do short/long MA cross bearishly?
Gives tech outlook: BEARISH/NEUTRAL/BULLISH

STEP 3 — ON-CHAIN & SENTIMENT (30% weight)
Asks: "What are holders doing? What's the sentiment?"
Checks:
  ✓ MVRV Z-Score — is BTC over/under valued?
  ✓ SOPR — are holders profitable or losing money?
  ✓ Exchange reserves — is supply leaving exchange (bullish)?
  ✓ Fear & Greed — extreme fear or greed?
Gives on-chain outlook: BEARISH/NEUTRAL/BULLISH

STEP 4 — DERIVATIVES (15% weight)
Asks: "Is there a short squeeze setup?"
Checks:
  ✓ Funding rates — are shorts crowded? (negative = yes)
  ✓ Open interest — is leverage at high levels?
  ✓ Liquidation heatmap — where do traders get liquidated?
Calculates squeeze probability (0-100%)

STEP 5 — COMBINE SCORES
Takes all 4 scores, weights them:
  = (macro×25%) + (tech×30%) + (onchain×30%) + (deriv×15%)
  = Final score determines signal

STEP 6 — GENERATE SIGNAL
If final score is very positive → BUY signal
If final score is very negative → SELL signal
If middle → HOLD signal
Confidence 0-92% based on score magnitude
Timeframe IMMEDIATE/NEXT_4H/WAIT based on confidence
