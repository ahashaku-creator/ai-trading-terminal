THE 6-STEP LOCAL AI ENGINE (implemented in JavaScript):

STEP 1: MACRO FILTER
  - Scores CPI, WTI oil, geopolitical risk, S&P 500
  - 25% weight in final signal calculation
  - Outputs: macroScore, macroOutlook

STEP 2: TECHNICAL CONFIRMATION  
  - Analyzes RSI, MACD, price vs SMAs, ADX
  - Checks for Death Cross pattern
  - 30% weight in final signal calculation
  - Outputs: techScore, techOutlook

STEP 3: ON-CHAIN & SENTIMENT
  - Evaluates MVRV Z-Score, LTH-SOPR, exchange reserves
  - Weighs Fear & Greed index (special: FG<20 = contrarian buy signal)
  - 30% weight in final signal calculation
  - Outputs: onchainScore, onchainOutlook

STEP 4: DERIVATIVES ANALYSIS
  - Analyzes funding rates (negative = crowded shorts = squeeze risk)
  - Evaluates open interest
  - Calculates short squeeze probability (0-100%)
  - 15% weight in final signal calculation
  - Outputs: derivScore, derivOutlook, squeezeRisk

STEP 5: SIGNAL COMPUTATION
  - Combines all scores with weighted average
  - Determines: BUY / SELL / HOLD signal
  - Calculates confidence 0-92% based on score magnitude
  - Special override: Extreme Fear + Crowded Shorts + Support = Force BUY
  - Determines timeframe: IMMEDIATE / NEXT_4H / WAIT

STEP 6: OUTPUT GENERATION
  - Generates human-readable prediction text
  - Creates specific trader action instructions
  - Calculates entry zone, target zone, stop loss
  - Computes risk/reward ratio
  - Builds 4-5 reasoning bullets from top factors
  - Compiles warning list for macro risks
